A little guidance

Make the most
of your workspace.

Everything you need to move from an idea to a little more progress.

How do I get started?

Create an account with your name, a unique username, email and password. Your overview will be ready straight away. Use “New project” to start your first idea.

How do projects and checklists work?

Give your project a name, add a description, choose a category and set an optional due date. On its detail page, add small next steps to the checklist. Tick them off as you go. Use “Edit project” to move it from Idea to In progress to Completed.

Checklist progress and project status are separate: finishing every checklist item does not change the status automatically. You decide when a project is complete.

Can I search or filter my work?

Open My projects and choose All, Idea, In progress or Completed. Search matches project names and descriptions. Your overview always shows your most recently updated projects.

Who can see my projects?

Your projects, profile and checklists are accessible through your signed-in account. There are no public project links or collaboration features in this version. The person operating the server controls its database and backups.

What if I forget my password?

Choose “Forgot password?” on the login page and enter your email. A reset link lasts 30 minutes and can be used once. When running the app locally with the default settings, the link appears in the terminal where the app is running. A hosted installation can deliver it by email once its operator configures SMTP.

How do I keep a copy of my work?

Open My profile and choose “Export my workspace”. This downloads your profile, projects, checklists and activity as a JSON file. It does not include passwords or password hashes.

How do I delete something?

Remove a checklist item with its × button. To delete a project, use “Delete project” on its detail page. Account deletion is under “Close your account” in My profile and requires your username and password. These actions are permanent.

How much can I add?

Each account supports up to 1,000 projects, with up to 100 checklist items per project. Descriptions can contain 5,000 characters. Use categories, search and filters to keep larger workspaces easy to explore.
