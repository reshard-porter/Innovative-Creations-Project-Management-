Here’s what this application stores and how you can manage it.

What is saved

Your account stores your name, username, email address, optional bio and a one-way password hash. Your workspace stores the projects, checklists and activity you create. Passwords are never displayed in your profile or saved as readable text.

Where your information goes

Information stays in the database configured by the person running this application. The default local setup stores it on that computer. A hosted installation stores it on its operator’s server or configured database. The operator is responsible for access, backups and retention.

The application includes no analytics, advertising trackers or external font services. If email recovery is configured, reset messages are sent through the operator’s email service. Local development recovery links are written to the server terminal instead.

Cookies and protection

A necessary session cookie keeps you signed in and supports form protection. It is marked HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax. Hosted production mode additionally requires HTTPS cookies. Password reset links expire after 30 minutes; changing a password invalidates existing sessions on other devices.

Your choices

Update your profile, change your password, export your workspace or permanently close your account from My profile. Deletion removes the live application records. The server operator controls whether older copies remain in their backups or logs.
